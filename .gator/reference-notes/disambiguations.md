# Reference Note: Disambiguations

Terms the operator uses that have common but incorrect default meanings. Consult this when a term could be misinterpreted.

| Term | Common default | Actual meaning in this Memex |
|------|---------------|------------------------------|
| **constitutional architecture** | U.S. Constitution / legal structure | Governance framework for AI-assisted development: constitutions, enforcers, role separation. See `constitution.md`. |
| **Memex** | Generic note-taking system | Specific architecture: tiered threads, annotated cross-references, constitutional governance, enforcer audit loops. Direct descendant of Bush 1945. |
| **Architect** (formerly PI) | Academic principal investigator | The human who holds mission, taste, and architectural coherence. Resolves tradeoffs the models cannot legitimately resolve on their own. Not a supervisor — a research director. Current term is "Architect"; legacy references to "PI" or "principal investigator" mean the same role. |
